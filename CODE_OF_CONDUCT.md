# Code of Conduct

nc-ui follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be patient, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not tolerated, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else the project is discussed.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, Peter Semmelhack (contact via https://psem.me/). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
